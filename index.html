I don't speak any German — do we really start from zero?

Yes, completely from zero. A1 is built for absolute beginners; we start with the alphabet and your first sentences.

Is 4.5 months really enough?

The plan assumes you attend regular weekly lessons and do the homework. If you stay consistent, B1 is a realistic goal — it's a clear system, not magic.

Is it 1-on-1 or group?

Both: 1-on-1 for your personal progress, small group for real speaking practice. The balance of the two is the fastest route.

What if I miss a lesson?

Missed lessons can be made up, but not back to back: if you miss one week, you shouldn't miss the next (make-up within two weeks). You also close the gap right away with the exclusive videos in your Premium library.

What can I actually do with B1?

B1 is the level required for residency, many job applications and most language exams in Germany. You can run daily life on your own.
