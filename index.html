I don't know any German — do we really start from zero?

Yes, completely from zero. A1 is built for absolute beginners; we start with the alphabet and your first sentences.

Is 4.5 months really enough?

The plan assumes you join regular weekly lessons and do the homework. With steady progress, B1 is a reachable goal — not magic, just a clear system.

1-on-1 or group?

Both: 1-on-1 for your personal progress, small group for real speaking practice. The balance of the two is the fastest way.

What if I miss a lesson?

It can be made up, but not back-to-back: if you miss one week you shouldn't miss the next (make-up at most every two weeks). Plus, Premium videos let you close the gap right away.

How do I pay?

Monthly in advance: 3 lessons a week €155/mo, 4 lessons a week €200/mo (lower per lesson). €14 per lesson; discounted in the monthly/package. First trial lesson free.

What is B1 good for?

B1 is the level required for residence, many job applications and most language exams in Germany. You can handle daily life on your own.
